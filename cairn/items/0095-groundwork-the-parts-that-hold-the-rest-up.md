Work with no user-visible result that the rest of the roadmap stands on: the shape of the
code, the shape of the tests, the tools that catch mistakes before a person does.

It exists because the alternative was `unfiled`, which is triage rather than a milestone
and was becoming a place where real work went to be forgotten. An item here is not
unscheduled; it is scheduled against the quality of everything else, which is a different
claim from "nobody has decided yet".

Nothing here changes what fontina does. It changes how quickly the next thing can, and
how likely that thing is to be right.
