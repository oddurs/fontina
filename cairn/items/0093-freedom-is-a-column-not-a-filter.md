## Problem

Whether you may study, change and pass on a face is a fact about it as basic as its
weight. In fontina it is `--free`, a filter you have to know to ask for, and a letter in
a flags column nobody has a legend for without reading `--help`.

For a project whose licence page argues the case at length, the program itself is quiet
about it.

## Proposal

Freedom appears where the weight appears — in the face table, in `info`, in the browser's
face pane — as a word rather than a letter. `list --free` stays.

`OS/2.fsType` stays reported and never enforced. It is the file's assertion about itself,
not a term of any licence, and acting on it would mean restricting somebody on their own
computer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The freedom verdict is visible without a flag, in the table and in `info`.
- [ ] It is a word, not a letter needing a legend.
- [ ] The reason is one keystroke or one flag away.
- [ ] Nothing is hidden, blocked or refused on the strength of it.
